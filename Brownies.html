BROWNIES



Ingredients

170 g (3/4cup) unsalted butter


200 g (7 oz) dark chocolate, chopped


250 g (1 and 1/4 cups) granulated sugar


2 large eggs, room temperature


1 egg yolk


1 tsp vanilla extract


95 g (3/4 cup) all-purpose flour


30 g (1/3 cup) unsweetened cocoa powder


1/2 tsp fine salt


Optional: 100 g (2/3 cup) chocolate chips or chunks



Procedures

1. Heat oven to 175°C / 350°F. Line the pan with baking paper, leaving some overhang.


2. Melt the butter and chopped chocolate together gently. Let cool until warm, not hot.


3. Whisk sugar, eggs, and yolk for about 2 minutes, until glossy and slightly thick. Whisk in vanilla and the melted chocolate mixture.


4. Sift in flour, cocoa, and salt. Fold just until no dry streaks remain. Fold in chocolate chunks if using.


5. Spread batter in the pan. Bake 24–30 minutes. The edges should look set; a toothpick in the center should come out with moist crumbs, not clean.


6. Cool completely in the pan before slicing. For extra chewy, fudgy squares, chill for an hour and bring to room temperature before serving.
