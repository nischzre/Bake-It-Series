MOIST CHOCOLATE CAKE



Ingredients

250 g (2 cups) all-purpose flour


65 g (¾ cup) unsweetened cocoa powder


300 g (1½ cups) granulated sugar


2 tsp baking powder


1½ tsp baking soda


1 tsp fine salt


2 large eggs


240 ml (1 cup) milk


120 ml (½ cup) neutral oil


2 tsp vanilla extract

240 ml (1 cup) hot coffee or hot water



Procedures

1. Heat oven to 175°C / 350°F. Grease and line two 8-inch (20-cm) round pans.


2. Whisk flour, cocoa, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a large bowl.


3. Add eggs, milk, oil, and vanilla. Beat until smooth, about 1–2 minutes.


4. Carefully stir in the hot coffee or water. The batter will be thin.


5. Divide between pans and bake 30–35 minutes, until a toothpick comes out with a few moist crumbs.


6. 1. Cool in the pans for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a rack and cool completely before frosting.
