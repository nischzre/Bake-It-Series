CHOCOLATE CHIP COOKIES



Ingredients

170 g (3/4 cup) unsalted butter, melted and cooled


200 g (1 cup, packed) brown sugar


100 g (1/2 cup) granulated sugar


1 large egg plus 1 egg yolk


2 tsp vanilla extract


250 g (2 cups) all-purpose flour


3/4 tsp baking soda


3/4 tsp fine salt


200 g (1¼ cups) chocolate chips or chopped chocolate



Procedures

1. Whisk the melted butter with both sugars. Whisk in the egg, yolk, and vanilla until smooth.


2. In a separate bowl, mix the flour, baking soda, and salt. Fold into the wet mixture just until combined, then fold in the chocolate.


3. Cover and chill the dough for at least 1 hour.


4. Heat oven to 175°C / 350°F. Line a baking sheet with baking paper. Scoop dough into balls, spacing them about 5 cm (2 inches) apart.


5. Bake for 10–12 minutes, until the edges are golden and the centers still look a little soft.


Let the cookies rest on the baking sheet for 5 minutes, then move them to a rack.
